## path-1
The village seems awfully quiet for the most part with its five dark, crooked houses. One of them has little sign on it with the word "KEEPER" in faded paint.

On your right, you see a warm, faint light spilling on the cobblestones. It feels caring, comforting, as if calling for your attention like a puppy wanting a warm hug. After closer inspection, you realize its a bakery.

Well? What will you do? The lighthouse wont light itself up, after all. 

[Knock on the bakery door](#bakery)
[Knock on the keeper's house](#old-keeper)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## bakery
You faintly knock on the door 3 times. Rustling noses fill the quiet until the door eventually opens. A woman is standing at the entrance with flour all over her clothes, with the cloudy culprit slowly starting to settle on the ground. A name tag with the name "Rosa" stays slightly crooked on the strap of her apron. 

"Hi! my name is Rosa. Ah sorry, I hope you don't mind the mess. Incase its not obvious, I'm the village baker in town." She says.

---After explaining your situation----

"Ohhh, you're the new keeper for the light house? Then you'll probably want to take this. The tower stairs are practically as black as a boot at night."

She hands you an old brass lantern.

get: lantern

"Oh! Before you leave, would.. you like to try some pie?" Rosa asks, politely. "There's a recipe I'm working on and I DESPERATELY need a taste tester. Its Blackberry, if you're interested"

[Stay for a slice of pie](#pie)
[Thank her and head back out](#path-1)

## pie
Well, who would dare say no to pie? You happily ablige, and with a smile on her face, Rosa cuts you a slice. She places it on a on the plate along with a small metal fork. 

"Here, come sit down. I'd feel bad if a guest stands in my home for too long." Rosa says, as she guides you to a chair by the fireplace.

You sit down on the soft, comfy chair and take a bite of the pie. It is absolutely delicious. With the buttery, flakey crust and the sweet, tart blackberry fills your tastebuds with a sense of joy. You close your eyes for just a moment to reslish the taste.

------------------

Something warm begins to wash over you, filling you with comfort. It feels like for the first time in a while, your soul filled with light. Wait...light?? What time is it?? You suddenly jerk awake, and see the sunrise from Rosa's window. It's morning. 

When you leave Rosa's house to check on things around the area, you spot the ship. Somehow, someway, the ship made it past the rocks by pure luck. The harbor master is waiting outside by the docks with a very long list of complaints.

THE END
