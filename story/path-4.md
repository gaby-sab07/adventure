## path-4
Up close, the lighthouse looms over you, far taller than it seemed from the crossroads. Wind rushes off the sea, carrying the sharp smell of salt, while waves crash against the rocks below. Its white paint peels in long curls, and the great lamp room at the top is dark—no welcoming beam to guide ships through the fog. The thick oak door is swollen with age, its iron lock spotted with rust.

[Unlock the door](#stairs) if iron key
[Knock and hope someone answers](#knock)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## knock
You knock. The sound echoes through the tower, bouncing from stone walls and up the spiral stair. It rings out over the dark sea and then disappears, swallowed by the wind. Nobody answers.

Of course nobody answers. You're the keeper, and the tower has been waiting for you to notice.

[Step back](#path-4)

## stairs
The key turns with a groan. Inside, a spiral staircase winds up into total darkness.

[Climb in the dark](#fall)
[Light your lantern and climb](#lamp-room) if lantern

## fall
Somewhere past step one hundred, your foot finds nothing. You sit down hard and decide to wait for morning.

The ship gets past the rocks, just barely. The next day, its captain sends you a very angry letter.

THE END

## lamp-room
At the top of the spiral stair, the great lamp sits inside a cage of glass prisms. One space in the ring is empty. Without that prism, the lamp can’t focus its light across the water.

[Give up and rest for the night](#give-up)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)
[Place the glass prism around the lamp](#light-lamp) if glass

## light-lamp
The valve resists. You brace one hand against the cold brass and turn again. Somewhere inside the lamp, a mechanism clicks.

Your lantern flame trembles against the wick. For one breath, nothing happens. Then the prisms catch fire with light, and a bright beam sweeps across the fog.

Far out at sea, a horn answers: two long blasts. ~*Thank you*~



THE END
