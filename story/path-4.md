## path-4
Up close, the lighthouse looms over you, far taller than it seemed from the crossroads. Wind rushes off the sea, carrying the sharp smell of salt, while waves crash against the rocks below. 

Its white paint peels in long curls, and the great lamp room at the top is dark—no welcoming beam to guide ships through the fog. The thick oak door is swollen with age, its iron lock spotted with rust.

## knock
You knock. The sound echoes through the tower, bouncing from stone walls and up the spiral stair. It rings out over the dark sea and then disappears, swallowed by the wind. Nobody answers.

Of course nobody answers. You're the keeper, and the tower has been waiting for you to notice.

[Step back](#path-4)

## stairs
The key grinds against the lock. For a moment, it won’t turn—then the mechanism gives with a sharp clack.

The door eases open. Cold air spills out, carrying the smell of wet stone and rust. A spiral staircase climbs into darkness, its steps vanishing overhead.

Somewhere above, metal scrapes against metal.

## explore-out
You circle the lighthouse, hunched against the wind. Rain slicks the stone, and the sea crashes below, sending spray up over the rocks.

You search the walls for a door, a window—anything. There’s nothing. Just weather-beaten stone, tangled weeds, and the lighthouse beam turning slowly overhead.

You’re about to head back when you notice the tower’s foundations stretch farther along the cliff than you expected.

## explore-deeper
At the tower’s seaward side, you spot a narrow iron vent half-hidden beneath ivy. Warm air breathes through it. When you press your hand to the stones, you feel a faint shudder from somewhere below.

You clear away the ivy and find a small iron ring set into the ground. It takes all your strength to pull. The stone hatch opens with a groan, releasing a breath of warm, stale air.

A ladder descends into the dark. Far below, something ticks steadily—then stops.

[Climb down](#secret-lab)
[Explore even more](#explore-more)
[Leave the hatch and go back to the lighthouse](#path-4)
[Leave the hatch and go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## explore-more
You leave the hatch alone and continue exploring around the lighthouse. Along the seaward wall, you find a narrow path between the tower and the cliff. It winds past rusted fixtures and patches of wind-bent grass, ending at a little lookout above the rocks.

From there, you can see the ship struggling through the fog. You search the lighthouse grounds for another way in, but find only a bricked-up doorway and old marks carved into the stone. The wind grows colder, and the hours pass as you follow the wall around the tower.

The ship gets past the rocks, just barely. The next day, its captain sends you a very angry letter.

THE END

## secret-lab
The ladder ends in a chamber buried beneath the lighthouse. Copper pipes crawl through the walls, and a blue light pulses from glass tubes mounted on iron stands. A workbench runs along one side of the room, crowded with brass instruments, jars of salt, and half-finished sketches of the coastline.

In the center of the chamber, a machine turns slowly by itself. Its needle trembles, then settles, pointing out toward the sea.

A thin layer of dust covers everything, but one thing stands out: a single glass prism, clear as a shard of moonlight, tucked beneath a cracked tray of lenses.

It is not broken. It is perfect.

## lab-mahine
You lean over the machine. Beneath its glass face, brass wheels turn in careful, intricate patterns. You follow their motion, trying to understand what the trembling needle is measuring.

Minutes slip by. Then hours. Each time you think you’ve found the answer, another gear shifts and reveals a new pattern. You barely notice the blue lights fading—or the first gray light of dawn creeping through the cracks above.

At last, the machine falls silent.

You scramble back to the surface. The sea is calm now, and it is morning. When you get back to the lighthouse, you see a letter on your desk. The captain of the ship sent it to you, and he is NOT happy.

THE END

## lamp-room
At the top of the spiral stair, the great lamp sits inside a cage of glass prisms. One space in the ring is empty. Without that prism, the lamp can’t focus its light across the water.

## give-up
You lay down on the cold stone floor beneath the lamp, too tired to keep fighting the dark. The tower settles around you: the groan of old beams, the hiss of the sea, the steady drip of water through a crack in the wall.

The fog rolls in from the harbor and swallows the last of the light. The lantern at your feet burns low, its flame trembling like a tired heart. For a moment, you think you hear a ship’s horn out at sea, but it may only be the wind rubbing against the rocks.

You close your eyes, and the night seems to close in with you. 

The ship gets past the rocks, just barely. The next day, its captain sends you a very angry letter.

THE END

## light-lamp
The valve resists. You brace one hand against the cold brass and turn again. Somewhere inside the lamp, a mechanism clicks.

Your lantern flame trembles against the wick. For one breath, nothing happens. Then the prisms catch fire with light, and a bright beam sweeps across the fog.

Far out at sea, a horn answers: two long blasts. ~*Thank you*~

THE END
